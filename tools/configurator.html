Connect the macropad

This page talks to the TISHRIC Knob firmware directly over USB. No drivers, nothing to install. Use Chrome or Edge.

In wheel mode (green LED in the middle) the macropad is an Xbox pad and can’t be configured. Hold the knob button for 3 seconds to switch it back to keyboard mode first.

Mode

The macropad reconnects after switching. In wheel mode this page can’t see it: to come back, hold the knob button for 3 seconds (the LEDs fill up yellow).
